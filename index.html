Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Tap two identical tiles that are free on at least one side and not covered by another tile. Remove every pair to clear the board.

I am stuck. What can I do?

Use the hint button to highlight a pair of tiles that can be matched.

What is the daily challenge?

Every day there is a new board for all players. Your result appears in the daily leaderboard under the nickname you chose.

How do I remove my nickname from the leaderboard?

Email us at the address above with the nickname you used, and we will remove it.
