Privacy Policy

Jongly Mahjong app · Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Jongly Mahjong app ("the app") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Data We Collect

  • Nickname and game results. If you take part in the daily challenge, the nickname you choose and your result are stored on our servers and shown to other players in the daily challenge leaderboard. Please do not use your real name or other personal information as a nickname.
  • Usage data. We collect anonymous information about how the app is used (for example, which screens are opened and which game modes are played) to understand and improve the app.
  • Device identifiers and advertising data. The app includes an advertising SDK. It may collect a device identifier and information about ads shown in the app.

The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location, and you do not need to create an account to play.

How We Use Data

  • To run the daily challenge leaderboard.
  • To analyze how the app is used and to improve it.
  • To display advertising in the app.

We do not sell your personal data.

Third-Party Services

The app uses services provided by Google, which process data on our behalf or as independent providers:

  • Firebase Analytics — app usage analytics
  • Cloud Firestore — storage of leaderboard nicknames and results
  • Google AdMob — advertising

These services handle data according to Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The app is suitable for a general audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will remove it.

Data Retention and Deletion

Leaderboard entries are kept for as long as they are needed to show the leaderboard. To have your nickname and results removed, email us at the address below and include the nickname you used. Game progress and settings stored on your device are deleted when you remove the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page.
